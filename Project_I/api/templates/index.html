🔬 What is Particulate Matter?

PM2.5 (Fine Particles): Particles with a diameter of 2.5 micrometers or less. Because they are so small, they can bypass lung filters and enter the bloodstream, causing respiratory and cardiovascular issues.

PM10 (Coarse Particles): Particles with a diameter of 10 micrometers or less. They irritate the eyes, nose, and throat, affecting people with asthma or allergies.

🚦 Air Quality Index (AQI) Reference

Good (0 - 50)

Air quality is satisfactory. No precautions needed.

Moderate (51 - 100)

Acceptable air quality. Unusually sensitive individuals should consider reducing heavy outdoor activities.

Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups (101 - 150)

Elderly, children, and people with heart/lung disease should wear masks and reduce prolonged outdoor exertion.

Unhealthy (151 - 200)

Everyone may begin to experience health effects. Wear protective masks (N95) outdoors and close windows.

Very Unhealthy (201 - 300)

Health alert: Active children, adults, and individuals with respiratory conditions should avoid all outdoor activities.

Hazardous (301+)

Health warnings of emergency conditions. The entire population is likely to be affected. Keep indoor air purifiers active.
